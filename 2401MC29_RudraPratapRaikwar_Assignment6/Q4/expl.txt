Q4 - Combined synchronization and deadlock avoidance

`syncdeadlock` creates five real xv6 children. The three shared resource
types are Printer (capacity 2), Scanner (capacity 1), and Disk (capacity 2).
Each child uses exactly two resource types and repeats its work for three
cycles.

The existing xv6 shared-memory and semaphore system calls are reused. Each
resource pool is represented by a counting semaphore. Every child acquires
its two resources in the global order Printer < Scanner < Disk, performs
work, and releases them. Because all acquisition follows one order, circular
wait cannot form. The completion counters in shared memory verify that every
child finishes all cycles.
